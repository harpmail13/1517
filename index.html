Current Project

Currently, I'm working on DeRM, an innovative magnetless motor design engineered to compete with PMRM (Permanent Magnet Rotary Motor) in efficiency... but at half the price. DeRM is designed to break the Neodymium supply monopoly and let EV manufacturers build sustainable, affordable motors via design licensing (similar to ARM computer chip IP).
