_Award: Swept both Best Use of 3D Printing & Best Use of Autodesk Software, [Make:able 2024](https://weareprintlab.com/blog/makeable-2024-winners/)_ * **Game Board For Kori (2025):** While no awards were won for this project, Kori still got a custom-designed busy board.
